# Gym Vocabulary (헬스장 단어)

Notes on Korean gym culture and vocabulary, collected from community discussions.

## Useful Phrases

| Korean | English |
|--------|---------|
| 몇 세트 남았어요? | How many sets do you have left? |
| (저는 ~ 할 건데) 좀 보조 해주실 수 있나요? | (I'm going to do a ...) Can you spot me? |
| 아직 쓰시고 있나요? | Are you still using this? |
| 하루에 몇 끼 먹나요? | How many meals do you eat a day? |

## Vocabulary

| Korean | English | Notes |
|--------|---------|-------|
| 기구 | machine/equipment | Preferred over 기계; people also just say 머신 |
| 헬창 | gym rat | Abbreviation of "health whore" -- used as a playful compliment among gym-goers, but can sound crude outside that context |
| 빵빵하다 | big/pumped (muscles) | e.g. 근육이 빵빵! |
| 체지방 | body fat | |
| 체중 | body weight | |
| 끼 | counter for meals | e.g. 하루에 몇 끼 먹나요? |
