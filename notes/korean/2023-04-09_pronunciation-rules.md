# Pronunciation Rules (발음 규칙)

## Consonant Shifts

1. **ㅅ (S) before 'i' or 'y' sounds becomes "SH"**
   - 시 is pronounced "shi" not "si"
   - 신문 (newspaper) sounds like "shin-mun"

2. **ㄹ (R/L) in the bottom of a syllable block (받침) is silent**
   - When ㄹ appears as the final consonant (받침), it is not pronounced in certain contexts
